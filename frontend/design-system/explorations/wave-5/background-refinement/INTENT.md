# Background refinement - Wave 5

## Intent

Take the approved Wave 4 system one step closer to a presentable external
reference without changing its product structure.

The main correction is the hero background. The previous implementation used
a grid and an oversized wheel pushed too far to the right. The validated visual
reference is cleaner: no grid, a large wheel centred within the hero composition,
and a softer optical/material treatment behind the existing copy and stats
ledger.

## Visual language

- **Wheel first:** a precise but low-contrast rim/spoke drawing acts as the main
  decorative anchor. It contains no labels or values and cannot be mistaken for
  product data.
- **No grid:** the page background is continuous and calm.
- **Continuation below the fold:** restrained concentric arcs and airflow-like
  curves appear near section edges. They remain low contrast and never sit on
  top of dense information.
- **Surface depth:** cards keep hairlines, subtle translucency and controlled
  shadows rather than flat white-on-white blocks.
- **Three themes, one system:** Light remains primary, with Cream and Dark as
  token translations of the same DOM and geometry.

## Product boundary

This is still a visual prototype. The comparator remains embedded in the
landing, with the validated left filter rail, scrollable Brand list, filter
switches, active chips and dense table. No new comparison logic, metrics,
product claims or navigation paths are introduced.

The wheel detail page is not revisited.
