Stop comparing PDFs and forum threads. Filter on the specs that actually matter for your ride: brand, price, weight, brake type, category, diameter, rim material and depth, tire compatibility, hubs, spokes. Each entry is structured once, from manufacturer pages, then served side by side so the numbers can be checked column against column.

“No marketing fluff, just numbers you can cross-check.”

From the data desk

Where a brand publishes separate front and rear values, both are carried as given. Variants appear as distinct rows rather than footnotes, and the minimum known price for each wheelset converts to your display currency at the switch of a selector.

Built with riders, manufacturers and resellers, the lab keeps its affiliations open. Figures are a point-in-time snapshot: prices are collected once and drift as shops adjust theirs. What follows is a specimen of the working sheet.
